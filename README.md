## BenzinApp
My old **thesis project** which I published to **[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.georgemc2610.benzinapp.xplatform.benzinapp)** and **[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gr/app/benzinapp/id6757866897)**! Written in Dart (Flutter), I managed to complete most of the features I wanted, and made it public!
A **Thesis Project** that I'm further adding features to it! <br>

This app is a great stats tracker for your cars, which can take in Fuel Fill Records and then automatically calculates:
+ Liters per 100 kilometers
+ Kilometers per Liter

## ML-Agents
Within the course of "Intelligent Agents", I was able to make with my team a really interesting **NPC kart that goes on its own!**

<img alt="kart-ia" width="32%" src="https://github.com/GeorgeMC2610/GeorgeMC2610/assets/55163372/ed111cd0-bfaa-4370-88d0-4469dfc5d70f" />

The kart is able to accelerate, steer and jump obstacles. Using the **Unity ML-Agents package**, we were able to train the kart to completely learn a circuit we created (The red circle is the start and the blue circle is the end).
